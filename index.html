Executive Summary

Why the Right
Gift Speaks Volumes

"A gift is not merely an object — it is a
declaration of values, crafted in silence."

In an era where genuine human connection has become the most coveted currency in business, a thoughtfully curated gift transcends transaction — it becomes a statement of intent, a silent ambassador of your organization's character. The brands that endure are not those with the largest advertising spend, but those remembered for how they made people feel.

At Minal Industres, we believe corporate gifting is a strategic discipline, not an afterthought. Every piece in this catalog has been vetted against three uncompromising standards: material integrity, functional longevity, and the power to surprise and delight someone who, frankly, has seen everything.

Whether you are onboarding a high-value client, retaining your most critical talent, or marking a milestone that deserves recognition — these selections are engineered to elevate your brand long after the ribbon is untied. This is gifting elevated to an art form.

Bespoke Services

Made Unmistakably
Yours

Every product in this catalog can be transformed into a bespoke brand expression. Below are our three pillars of customization — each executed with the same exactitude we apply to every product we curate.

Precision Logo Engraving & Embossing

Your corporate mark, rendered with surgical precision via laser engraving, deep embossing, or UV spot-varnish on metal, leather, wood, ceramic, and glass surfaces. We work from vector files and produce a physical strike-test sample for your approval before any bulk production commences.

  • Laser engraving on metal, glass, wood
  • Blind or foil-stamp embossing on leather
  • UV spot varnish on paper & card
  • Pantone-matched screen-printing on textiles
  • Debossed wax seal on packaging
Sample strike approval included. Typically 3–5 business days.

Custom Packaging & Unboxing Architecture

The unboxing is the first impression — and in gifting, there is no second chance. Our packaging atelier designs custom rigid boxes, slipcovers, and shipping mailers with brand-coherent color, texture, and structural integrity that rivals luxury retail. We provide 3D renders for approval before tooling begins.

  • Bespoke rigid box in any Pantone color
  • Custom interior foam or die-cut paper inserts
  • Branded tissue paper, ribbon & wax seals
  • Outer shipper with branded corrugated print
  • QR-coded gift experience landing page
3D render approval + physical sample before production.

Personalized Note Cards & Calligraphy

A machine-generated signature is noticed. A genuine handwritten word is remembered. Our network of professional calligraphers scripts individual recipient names and personal messages on 320 gsm cotton letterpress stock at up to 800 cards per day, matched with a branded envelope in your corporate color and sealed with a wax emblem in your mark.

  • Calligraphy in 6 script styles (from Copperplate to modern minimal)
  • Variable data printing for large personalization batches
  • CEO / Founder signature facsimile on 220 gsm stock
  • Branded envelopes in 12 base colors + custom liner
  • Wax seal in corporate monogram or custom motif
Calligraphy available at 800 pieces/day. Lead 5–7 days.

All bespoke services are quoted individually. Request a Customization Consultation with our Brand Experience team.
