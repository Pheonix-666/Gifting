Executive Summary

Why the Right
Gift Speaks Volumes

"A gift is not merely an object — it is a declaration of values, crafted in silence."

In an era where genuine human connection has become the most coveted currency in business, a thoughtfully curated gift transcends transaction — it becomes a statement of intent, a silent ambassador of your organization's character.

At Minal Industres, we believe corporate gifting is a strategic discipline, not an afterthought. Every piece in this catalog has been vetted against three uncompromising standards: material integrity, functional longevity, and the power to surprise and delight someone who, frankly, has seen everything.

Whether you are onboarding a high-value client, retaining your most critical talent, or marking a milestone that deserves recognition — these selections are engineered to elevate your brand long after the ribbon is untied.

Bespoke Services

Made Unmistakably
Yours

Every product in this catalog can be transformed into a bespoke brand expression. Below are our three pillars of customization — each executed with the same exactitude we apply to every product we curate.

Precision Logo Engraving & Embossing

Your corporate mark rendered with surgical precision via laser engraving, deep embossing, or UV spot-varnish on metal, leather, wood, ceramic, and glass surfaces.

  • Laser engraving on metal, glass, wood
  • Blind or foil-stamp embossing on leather
  • UV spot varnish on paper & card
  • Pantone-matched screen-printing on textiles
  • Debossed wax seal on packaging
Sample strike approval included · Typically 3–5 business days

Custom Packaging & Unboxing Architecture

The unboxing is the first impression. Our packaging atelier designs custom rigid boxes, slipcovers, and mailers with brand-coherent color, texture, and structural integrity.

  • Bespoke rigid box in any Pantone color
  • Custom interior foam or die-cut inserts
  • Branded tissue paper, ribbon & wax seals
  • Outer shipper with branded corrugated print
  • QR-coded gift experience landing page
3D render approval + physical sample before production

Personalized Note Cards & Calligraphy

A genuine handwritten word is remembered. Our calligraphers script individual recipient names and messages on 320gsm cotton stock at up to 800 cards per day.

  • Calligraphy in 6 script styles
  • Variable data printing for large batches
  • CEO / Founder signature facsimile on 220gsm
  • Branded envelopes in 12 colors + custom liner
  • Wax seal in corporate monogram or motif
800 pieces/day · Lead time 5–7 business days
